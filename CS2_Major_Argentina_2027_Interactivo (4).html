04 — Sales-Driven Campaign

Retail Sell-Out with CompraGamer

The 100 signed collector units — 90 from Setup A and 10 from Setup B (used in the Final) — are sold post-event exclusively through CompraGamer, Argentina's largest gaming e-tailer — turning the Major into a direct retail activation, not just a media moment.

Exclusive Retail Partner

Argentina's #1 gaming-focused e-commerce platform, with the largest established base of PC gaming and hardware buyers in the country — the natural home for a limited, collector-grade AORUS drop.

Exclusive Drop100 signed, numbered AORUS Major collector PCs (90 Setup A + 10 Setup B Final units) — sold only on CompraGamer, nowhere else.
Major-Timed LaunchSale opens around the tournament's closing moment, riding the peak of hype and media coverage.
Frictionless CheckoutCompraGamer's native installment financing ("cuotas sin interés") lowers the barrier for a premium-priced collector item.
Cross-PromotionCompraGamer's newsletter, social channels and homepage placements amplify AORUS and the Major to their full gamer audience.
Bundle UpsellOptional AORUS peripheral bundles (mouse, keyboard, monitor) offered at checkout to lift average order value.
Lead CaptureEvery purchase and waitlist signup feeds a qualified gamer database for future AORUS retargeting in Argentina.

Why CompraGamer

  • Direct access to Argentina's largest concentration of PC gaming buyers, without building retail infrastructure from scratch
  • Established payments, logistics and warranty support de-risk selling a high-value, limited-run collector product
  • Local credibility and trust reduce friction for a premium-priced drop tied to a first-time event in the country
  • Reinforces AORUS's premium positioning by association with the country's leading gaming retail destination
Ties Directly To The Recovery Estimate
The $249,828 estimated recovery in the Investment section (~$2,498 per unit across 100 units) is the resale plan this campaign is built to deliver.
